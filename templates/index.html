SCSVMV

Sri Chandrasekharendra Saraswathi Viswa Mahavidyalaya, also known as Kanchi University, is located in Enathur, Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu.

The university offers programmes in multiple academic areas. Programme availability and admission details should be confirmed for the current academic year.
